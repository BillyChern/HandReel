Data credits

The input frames shown in the viewer (viewer/input/, the input frames inside viewer/recordings/ and the pre-rendered
videos in viewer/fallback/) and the frames inside the page's figures (fig/) come from the datasets below. Each
dataset entry gives the licence that the dataset states, the assets that hold its frames and the changes made to
those frames.

3D content
  Object: the object mesh, its scale and its poses are HandReel's output on those frames; the mesh is generated
    from the keyframe by SAM 3D Objects (https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-objects), which Meta releases
    under the SAM License.
  Hand: the hand is HaWoR's output, unchanged (HaWoR, https://hawor-project.github.io/; its code and models are
    released under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0), as a mesh of the MANO hand model (https://mano.is.tue.mpg.de/; licence:
    https://mano.is.tue.mpg.de/license.html).

Viewer software
  The interactive viewer (viewer/client/) is the static client of Viser 1.1.1 (https://viser.studio/), released
  under the Apache License 2.0. It bundles code from other packages, among them three.js, React, Mantine,
  react-three-fiber, drei, camera-controls, troika-three-text, zustand and the unified/hast/micromark family,
  released under the MIT, ISC, BSD, 0BSD and Apache-2.0 licences. The full licence texts and copyright notices
  of Viser and of every bundled package are in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt, served beside this file.
  The client carries one environment map, used to light the meshes: Viser's default preset "city", the file
  potsdamer_platz_1k.jpg of the Viser 1.1.1 package (37,111 bytes, sha256 01e4690f086a...), read from inside the
  client and from no other host. The Viser package ships it without a licence note of its own.

Chart in the section "Reconstruction time"
  No dataset media. The bars and labels are numbers that the paper's Table 2 prints, measured on Ego4D video
  (Ego4D, Grauman et al., "Ego4D: Around the World in 3,000 Hours of Egocentric Video", 2022). The page shows
  no Ego4D frame or clip.
